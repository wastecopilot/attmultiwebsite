Samsung Galaxy Z Flip8 and Z Fold8 Series Trade-in Offer

Trade-In Offer Details

Eligibility: New and existing customers activating a new line or upgrading an existing line. 
Discounts:  
Up to $1,200 off Galaxy Z Flip8, Z Fold8, Z Fold8 Ultra with activation on AT&T Premium 2.0 plan or higher (starting at $100/mo. plus taxes and fees, before discounts for single line) or select retired/legacy unlimited plans and trade-in of an eligible smartphone with min. trade-in value of $95 or more (up to $1,000 off with trade-in value of $35-$94).
Up to $1,000 off eligible devices with activation on AT&T Extra 2.0 plan (starting at $80/mo. plus taxes and fees, before discounts for single line) and trade-in of an eligible smartphone with min. trade-in value of $95 (up to $800 off with trade-in value of $35-$94). Customers on legacy Starter/Starter SL plans qualify for a max of $800 off eligible devices with trade-in of eligible smartphone with min. trade-in value of $35 or more.
Up to $500 off eligible devices with activation on AT&T Value 2.0 plan (starting at $60/mo. plus taxes and fees, before discounts for single line) and trade-in of eligible smartphone with min. trade-in value of $35 or more.
Purchase Requirements: Requires qualifying 0% APR 36-month installment plan with monthly payments of up to $75; based on eligible device purchased. $0 down is available for well-qualified customers; others may require a down payment. Other installment options may vary by account type. Customers must pay tax on full retail price and an activation/upgrade fee of $35 per line. 
Credits: Credits applied over installment term, starting within 3 bills. If the wireless plan on the purchased device is lowered during the installment term, credits may stop or be reduced to the max avail. for the lower plan, if eligible. Canceling wireless service stops credits and remaining device balance is due. If adding a new line, must not cancel service on any other line within 90 days or credits may stop. Early payoff or upgrade may stop credits. Max credit will not exceed device cost or eligible credit amt.
Plan Limitations/Restrictions: This offer requires an eligible postpaid unlimited voice & data plan. AT&T may temporarily slow data speeds if the network is busy.  AT&T 55+, Value Plus, Value Plus VL, and Value Plus Unlimited plans are not eligible. See att.com/unlimited for current unlimited plans. 
Offer and requirements subject to change at any time and without notice.
