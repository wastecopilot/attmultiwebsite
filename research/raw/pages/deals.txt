Trade-In Offer Details 

Eligibility: New and existing customers activating a new line or upgrading an existing line.  
Discounts:   
Up to $1,200 off iPhone 18 Pro and iPhone 18 Pro Max and iPhone Duo with activation on AT&T Premium 2.0 plan or higher (min. $100/mo. plus taxes and fees, before discounts for single line) or select retired/legacy unlimited plans and trade-in of an eligible smartphone with min. trade-in value of $230 or more.  
Up to $930 off iPhone 18 Pro, iPhone 18 Pro Max, iPhone Duo, and iPhone 17 with activation on AT&T Extra 2.0 plan or higher (min. $80/mo. plus taxes and fees, before discounts for single line) and trade-in of an eligible smartphone with min. trade-in value of $130 or more. Customers on AT&T Extra 2.0 or legacy AT&T Extra, Starter/SL plans qualify for a max credit of $930 with eligible trade-in of $130 or more.  
Max credit of up to $500 off all eligible devices with activation on Value 2.0 plan and trade-in of an eligible smartphone with min. trade-in value of $130 or more. 
Max credit of up to $350 off all eligible devices with activation on any eligible unlimited plan and trade-in of an eligible smartphone with min. trade-in value of $35-$129. 
Purchase Requirements: Requires 0% APR 36-month installment plan with monthly payments of up to $88.89; based on eligible device purchased. $0 down is available for well-qualified customers; others may require a down payment. Other installment options may vary by account type. Customers must pay tax on full retail price and an activation/upgrade fee of $35 per line.  
Credits:  Credits applied over installment term, starting within 3 bills. If the wireless plan on the purchased device is lowered during the installment term, credits may stop or be reduced to the max avail. for the lower plan, if eligible. Canceling wireless service stops credits and remaining device balance is due.  If adding a new line, must not cancel service on any other line within 90 days or credits may stop. Early payoff or upgrade may stop credits. Max credit will not exceed device cost or eligible credit amt. 
Plan Limitations/Restrictions: This offer requires an eligible postpaid unlimited voice & data plan. AT&T may temporarily slow data speeds if the network is busy. AT&T 55+, Value Plus, Value Plus VL, and Value Plus Unlimited plans are not eligible. See att.com/unlimited for current unlimited plans.  
Offer and requirements subject to change at any time and without notice.

Additional Details 

Trade-In Devices:
Eligibility: The trade-in device must not be on an existing installment plan must be completed within 30 days from the start of the new smartphone's installment plan. For online orders or when the new device must be shipped, installment plan and 30-day Trade-in window start when the new purchased device is shipped. 
Eligible Trade-in Devices: Eligible trade-in devices are subject to change at any time and without notice.  For terms and to check if your device is eligible, visit tradein.att.com.
