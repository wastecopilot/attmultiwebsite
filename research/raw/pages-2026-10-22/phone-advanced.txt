AT&T Phone – Advanced

How does it work?

Instead of traditional wired phone lines, AT&T Phone – Advanced uses the AT&T cellular network and/or a broadband connection to provide your home telephone service. Once everything is set up, it works just like your traditional phone does now. Most importantly, you’ll enjoy a crystal-clear, reliable connection.

We make it easy to get set up

Professional installation

We’ll get you started

Once you receive your device, you can schedule an appointment for an AT&T technician to visit your home to set it up for an additional charge. They’ll also help set up your previously connected devices.

Self-install

Do it yourself

Your AT&T Phone – Advanced device will come with everything you need to set up and get connected. Colored stickers label each component and match each step in the installation guide for hassle-free setup.

Two ways to keep you connected
Cellular connection

AT&T Phone – Advanced uses the AT&T cellular network to provide voice service. There’s no separate charge for this connectivity. Just be sure to place your device on the first or second floor near a window or outside wall.

Home broadband connection

AT&T Phone – Advanced works with most broadband connection. You’ll simply use your current modem. If the AT&T cellular network connection is unavailable, you’ll stay connected via broadband.  Just be sure the device is located in a convenient location (not in a closet, basement, or other hard-to-reach-location).
