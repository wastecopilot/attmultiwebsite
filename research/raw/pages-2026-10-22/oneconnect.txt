AT&T OneConnect
Unlimited 5G wireless and 1 Gig fiber internet, on one plan with one all-in price.
Get started
Learn more
How is OneConnect different than other plans?

Unlimited high-speed 5G wireless1

1 Gig fiber internet2

One flat price
Taxes and fees included.

Phone and data lines included
Bring your additional devices at no extra cost.3

No disruptions
Keep your old services until your new ones are connected.

Internet backup included
 If your internet goes down, your wireless data will step up.4

Add-ons available
Add 50GB hotspot data for $15/mo. per line.5

Wi-Fi extenders included
Enjoy coverage across your entire home.

1Requires compatible device. Coverage not available everywhere. Learn more at att.com/5Gforyou. 2Limited availability in select areas. 3Max number of lines varies by plan; min 1 required. Requires eSIM compatible devices. 4Requires activation and power source; speeds vary; AT&T may slow data speeds if the network is busy. Backup may not be available in all locations. 5After 50GB mobile hotspot data, speeds slowed to max. 128Kbps.

How it works

OneConnect is only available to new customers with eligible, unlocked eSIM devices.

Choose a plan

Pick the one that fits your needs. Add additional lines up to your limit at no extra cost.

Buy & register

Provide your contact, service, and billing information, then click Purchase. After that, click Register or follow the email instructions to set up your account.

Set up your account

OneConnect requires you to bring your own, unlocked phone. Once you set up your first phone line, schedule your fiber installation.
