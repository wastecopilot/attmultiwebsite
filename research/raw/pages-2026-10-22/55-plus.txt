Add home internet

Get two lines of unlimited wireless
Includes unlimited talk, text, and data in the U.S., Canada, and Mexico, plus 10GB hotspot data per line and standard-definition streaming.

AT&T may temporarily slow data speeds if the network is busy.

Protection and backup included
Internet service includes AT&T ActiveArmor®. Fiber customers may also get AT&T Internet Backup, so you’ll always stay connected.
Stay connected with home internet
Where available, add AT&T Fiber 300Mbps or AT&T Internet Air with unlimited data and no equipment fees.
No annual contract
Enjoy the flexibility to adjust your service without long-term commitments.
